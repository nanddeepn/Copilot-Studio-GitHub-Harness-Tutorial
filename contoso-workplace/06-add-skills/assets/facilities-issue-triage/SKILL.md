# Facilities issue triage

Use this skill when an employee reports a non-emergency issue with a workplace space or facility.

## Procedure

1. Identify the building, floor, room or area, observed issue, and when it began. Ask one focused question for the most important missing detail; do not repeat information already provided.
2. Search the approved facilities knowledge sources. If no relevant procedure is available, say so and help prepare a concise report for the facilities team. Do not invent repair instructions, response times, emergency procedures, or service commitments.
3. For routine issues, summarize only the safe observations and details that the employee can provide. Do not tell the employee to repair, disassemble, or handle electrical, plumbing, structural, or hazardous equipment.
4. If the employee reports immediate danger, smoke, sparking, burning odor, flooding near electrical equipment, or another apparent emergency, stop routine triage. Tell them to follow the site's emergency procedures and contact on-site staff or emergency services as appropriate. Do not delay emergency response to gather more details.
5. For non-emergency issues, prepare a short summary containing the location, observed issue, when it began, and any relevant safety observations. Do not imply the issue was filed unless an authorized workflow confirms the notification.
6. End when the employee has a useful summary or has been directed to the appropriate local response.

Do not collect access codes, passwords, health information, or unrelated personal details.
